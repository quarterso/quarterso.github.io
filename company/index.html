Finance for the agentic era

Agents now spend company money on their own. The tools that control company spending were built for people with cards. We are building the one built for agents.

Why now

  • Agents spend on their own

    They call paid APIs, buy data and pay other software, many times a minute, with no one watching each call.

  • Cards were built for people

    A card sees one monthly charge from an AI vendor. It cannot stop the call that caused it, or say which agent made it.

  • Mistakes are expensive

    A loop can burn a budget overnight. A wrong command can delete a production database.

What we believe

  • Approve before, not after

    An agent should never spend or delete what no one allowed. Rules must hold before the money moves.

  • Neutral

    Every AI vendor, every card, every way agents pay. We are not one vendor’s gateway or one bank’s card.

  • Simple to adopt

    Change a base URL and a key. Nothing else in your agents changes.

The plan. Each step starts once teams use the one before it.

  1. 01

    The control layer

    Budgets, approvals and the signed record for every agent call and payment. Built, in pre-release.

  2. 02

    Quarter cards

    A card per agent or vendor, issued through a licensed partner, approved charge by charge.

  3. 03

    Agent accounts

    Balances agents spend from, held at a partner bank, with the same rules on every payment.
