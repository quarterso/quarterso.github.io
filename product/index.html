Spend management for AI agents

Quarter approves every call and payment before the money moves, on every vendor and rail, and closes the books on it. It runs on your own server.

Budgets. Rules that hold before the money moves, not alerts after the bill.

  • Caps before spend

    One budget for everything, or caps per agent, task and customer. A call that would break one never leaves.

  • Approvals

    A call above your line waits for a person, in Slack, the dashboard or the terminal.

  • Loop stops

    An agent stuck repeating itself, or spending ten times faster than usual, is paused.

  • Cheaper model near a cap

    A call that would break a cap can move to a cheaper model you allowed.

Every vendor and rail. One policy, wherever the money goes.

  • Every AI vendor and API

    OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, and any other API with a base URL.

  • Coding agents

    Claude Code and Codex, subscriptions included. Company machines can be set to send them only through Quarter.

  • Agent payments

    x402, MPP, cards and invoices get the same caps and approvals. Your own wallet or card pays, only once Quarter signs off.

  • Known payees

    Paying someone new waits for a person.

Safe by default. Agents get a budget, not the keys.

  • No real keys in agents

    Each agent holds a Quarter key. Quarter adds the real key on the way out.

  • Destructive actions wait

    Deleting data, dropping a table, force-pushing or tearing down servers waits for a person.

  • Personal data masked

    Emails, card numbers and other personal data are hidden from the model and put back in the reply.

Books and proof. Finance closes on it. Auditors can check it.

  • Live dashboard

    Spend, caps, approvals waiting, and where the month will land.

  • Month-end journal

    Spend by agent, task, customer and vendor, in a journal that always balances, ready to import into QuickBooks, Xero or NetSuite.

  • A record no one can quietly edit

    Every entry is chained to the one before it, and every approval names the person who gave it.

  • Audit pack

    One export a month, mapped to AIUC-1, SOC 2 and the EU AI Act.

The dashboard. Spend, caps, approvals and the costliest tasks in one place.

Where it is going. Software first. Money movement through licensed partners, once teams use it.
