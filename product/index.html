Control, pay and close

Quarter approves agent spend before the money moves, on every rail, and books it to the right customer at month end. It runs on your own server.

Control. A spend policy that holds before the money moves, not an alert after the bill.

  • Budgets

    By agent, task and customer, by day or month. A call that would exceed one never reaches the vendor.

  • Approvals

    Spend above your threshold waits for a named approver, in Slack or the dashboard.

  • Runaway spend stopped

    An agent repeating itself, or spending ten times its usual rate, is paused.

  • Coding agents

    Claude Code and Codex fall under the same budgets as every other agent.

Pay. One policy, wherever the money goes.

  • AI vendors and APIs

    OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, and any API with a base URL.

  • Agent cards

    Cards you issue through Stripe Issuing. Quarter approves or declines each swipe against the same budgets.

  • Agent payments

    x402 and MPP payments are authorised before they are signed. The seller gets signed proof your policy allowed them.

  • Known payees

    Paying a new merchant or wallet waits for a person.

Close. AI spend in the books, by customer, without a spreadsheet.

  • Margin by customer

    Spend that served a customer is booked as cost of revenue. Add your revenue and see each customer’s margin after AI cost, worst first.

  • Month-end journal

    A balanced entry with the accrual, as a file QuickBooks, Xero or NetSuite imports as it is.

  • A record that holds

    Every entry is chained to the one before it, and every approval carries the approver’s name.

  • Audit pack

    One export a month for your auditors, mapped to SOC 2 and AIUC-1.

The dashboard. Spend against budget, approvals waiting, and cost by customer.

Where it is going. Software first. Money movement through licensed partners, once teams use it.
