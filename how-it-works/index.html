Checked before it is spent

Every agent call works like a card authorisation: Quarter holds the most it could cost, checks it against your policy, and settles it at the real cost.

One call, five steps

  1. 01

    Request

    The agent calls Quarter instead of the vendor, with its Quarter key and the customer it is working for.

  2. 02

    Hold

    Quarter works out the most the call could cost and holds that amount against the budget.

  3. 03

    Check

    Budgets, pauses, rules and the approval threshold, in one write to the ledger, so parallel calls cannot overspend.

  4. 04

    Forward

    Quarter adds the real vendor key on the way out. The agent never holds it.

  5. 05

    Settle

    The hold is replaced by the cost the vendor reports, booked to the agent, task and customer.

What the agent gets back. Nothing that fails a rule reaches the vendor.

AnswerMeaning
200Allowed. The vendor’s reply, passed through as it is.
402Over a cap. The reply says which.
403 approval_requiredNeeds approval. Retry with the approval id once it is approved, or wait and the call goes on.
403 rule_deniedAn egress rule forbids the call. The reply names the rule.
423The agent or task is paused by a loop stop, until a person resumes it.
401Missing or wrong Quarter key.
